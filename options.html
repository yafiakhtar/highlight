Guide

Learn the basics, organize your highlights, and understand what can and cannot be undone.

Quick Start

Save your first highlight in three steps.

  1. 1 Select some text

    Drag across a useful sentence or idea on any webpage.

  2. 2 Choose how to save it

    Pick a tag color from the floating button, or use your keyboard shortcut.

  3. 3 Find it in Library

    Your highlight is saved automatically and grouped with its webpage.

Ways to Highlight

Use whichever method feels most natural.

  • Floating action button Select text and choose a tag color. If Folder or Comment is in your layout, highlight first and then use that action while the button remains open. Favorite can save a default-tag favorite immediately.
  • Keyboard shortcut Use the shortcut shown in Settings to quickly save selected text with your default tag.
  • Right-click menu Select text, right-click, and choose the Highlight command when you do not want to use the floating button.
  • Existing highlights Click one to reopen its floating button, drag across it to select or copy its text, or right-click it without a selection to move it to Recently Deleted.

Organize Your Highlights

Use each tool for a different kind of organization.

  • TagsEvery highlight has one tag. Its tag controls its name and color, and changing a tag preset updates every highlight using it.
  • FavoritesStar the highlights you want to reach quickly. Favoriting does not change their tag, folder, or comment.
  • FoldersA highlight can be in one folder or left unfiled. Folders are useful for projects, classes, or topics.
  • CommentsAdd one short note to remember why a passage mattered. You can create or edit it from the webpage or Library.

Using the Library

Choose a view based on what you are looking for.

  • AllEvery saved highlight, grouped by webpage.
  • FavoritesOnly the highlights you have starred.
  • TagsBrowse highlights by their name and color.
  • CommentsReview highlights that contain notes.
  • FoldersCreate, rename, and browse your folders.
  • Recently DeletedRestore removed highlights or delete them forever.

Customize Your Setup

Make highlighting fit the way you read and organize.

  • Choose your colorsEdit the default tag in Appearance or manage the complete palette in Tag Presets. Existing highlights update with their tag.
  • Build your floating buttonAdd, remove, drag, and reorder up to eight tag and action buttons. Close, Favorite, Folder, and Comment are optional.
  • No manual saving neededChanges made in Settings save automatically.

Before You Delete

Know what can be restored and what is permanent.

ActionConfirmationWhat happens
  • Delete a normal highlightRight-click it on a webpage, or use its Library row
    No dialog Moves to Recently Deleted and can be restored.
  • Clear one webpage in LibraryUse Clear all beside that webpage
    No dialog Moves that webpage’s highlights to Recently Deleted.
  • Clear the current webpageUse the trash button in the popup
    Asks first Moves the page’s highlights to Recently Deleted.
  • Delete a commentThe highlight remains saved
    Asks first Permanently removes only the comment.
  • Delete a folderYou choose what happens to its highlights
    Asks first Keeps highlights unfiled or moves them to Recently Deleted.
  • Delete a custom tagBuilt-in default tag cannot be deleted
    No dialog Removes the tag; affected highlights use the default tag.
  • Reset settingsLibrary records stay saved
    Asks first Restores default tags and FAB settings without deleting highlights.
  • Delete one item foreverFrom Recently Deleted
    No dialog Permanently deletes that highlight and cannot be undone.
  • Empty Recently DeletedDeletes every item inside it
    Asks first Permanently deletes everything after confirmation.

Good to Know

A few details that make the extension easier to understand.

  • Your highlights, folders, comments, and preferences currently stay in this Chrome browser profile.
  • Cross-device Sync is planned but is not available yet.
  • Copy Link and Share appear in the FAB Builder as coming-soon actions and do not work yet.
  • Renaming or recoloring a tag updates every highlight connected to that tag.
